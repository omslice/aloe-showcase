# AI, Privacy, and Human Oversight

ALOE uses AI to help a person research, organize, evaluate, and prepare outreach. It is designed as a decision-support system—not an autonomous messaging agent.

## AI-assisted workflows

- **Evidence synthesis:** Turns scattered, permission-compatible source material into concise company and contact summaries while retaining supporting evidence.
- **Lead prioritization:** Assesses relevance, timing, reachability, audience size, growth signals, and evidence quality to support—not replace—human judgment.
- **Contact verification:** Helps determine whether a person, role, profile, and organization appear to belong together; ambiguous matches are flagged for review.
- **Personalized draft preparation:** Creates context-aware outreach drafts grounded in approved project information and saved evidence.
- **Reply and experiment analysis:** Organizes responses, suggests follow-up states, and compares message variants.

## Human oversight

- No autonomous mass outreach.
- No message is sent merely because an AI system recommended it.
- A person reviews the recipient, evidence, tone, channel, and final wording before external communication.
- Missing information should be identified rather than invented.
- Important conclusions should remain traceable to source evidence.

## Public repository boundary

This repository intentionally contains product documentation and reviewed, non-sensitive media only. It does not contain private application source, model credentials, private prompts, lead databases, contact details, outreach histories, operational configuration, or automation secrets.
