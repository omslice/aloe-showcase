# Public Roadmap

This roadmap communicates product direction without exposing private implementation details or operational data.

## Now

- Improve source-health reporting and recovery guidance.
- Strengthen contact and organization confidence checks.
- Make ranking evidence easier to inspect.
- Refine reviewable, human-approved outreach batches.
- Prepare privacy-reviewed screenshots for the product gallery.

## Next

- Improve cross-source company identity resolution.
- Expand reply classification and follow-up organization.
- Add clearer experiment comparisons for message variants.
- Improve bounded workflow scheduling and audit visibility.
- Add privacy-preserving operational summaries and exports.

## Later

- Broaden permission-compatible source coverage.
- Add more configurable evaluation criteria.
- Improve explainability for prioritization recommendations.
- Publish additional product documentation and demonstrations.

Priorities may change as the product is tested. This public roadmap deliberately omits proprietary architecture, source configuration, and private operational details.
